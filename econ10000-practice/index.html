Budget line, indifference curves, and the price-change split

The optimum sits where the indifference curve is tangent to the budget line: MUx/MUy = Px/Py. Raise Px to see the move from A to C (substitution) and C to B (income).

Inputs

Preferences here are Cobb-Douglas, U = xay1−a, so X is always a normal good. For an inferior good the income effect would point the other way and the order becomes A, B, C.

Linear demand: elasticity, revenue, consumer surplus

For P = A − B·Q, own-price elasticity is |E| = (1/B)(P/Q). It falls from infinity at the top to 1 at the midpoint to 0 at the bottom. Revenue peaks where |E| = 1.

Inputs

Start at P = 20. Cut the price a little. Does revenue go up or down? Now try the same thing at P = 8.
